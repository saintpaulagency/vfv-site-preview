Car donation in Oregon

Vehicles For Veterans picks up donated cars, trucks, boats, RVs and motorcycles across Oregon at no cost to you. Proceeds go to charities that help disabled veterans, and you get an IRS tax receipt for your records.

How it works

Three steps, and the free pickup comes to you.

  1. Tell us about your vehicle

    Pick the vehicle type, add your pickup ZIP and contact details. It's three short screens.

  2. Schedule your free pickup

    The nearest auction partner contacts you to set a pickup time that works for you.

  3. Get your tax receipt

    You receive an IRS tax receipt for your records after the vehicle sells.

Questions donors ask

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Car Donation Foundation is a tax-exempt charity under section 501(c)(3) of the IRS Code, and you receive an IRS tax receipt for your records. Ask your tax advisor how the deduction applies to you.

Does my vehicle need to run?

No. Pickup is free whether the vehicle runs or not.

What can I donate?

Cars, trucks, motorcycles, boats, RVs, campers and ATVs.

We are a nonprofit

Vehicles For Veterans is Car Donation Foundation's vehicle donation program. Car Donation Foundation is a tax-exempt charity under section 501(c)(3) of the IRS Code and donates the proceeds from this program to other tax-exempt charities that help disabled veterans.

Car Donation Foundation also runs Wheels For Wishes, benefiting Make-A-Wish; Wheels For Wishes & Wellness, benefiting local children's hospitals and charities; and Animal Car Donation, benefiting animal rescue organizations.
